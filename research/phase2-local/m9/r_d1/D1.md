## What this means for HSD

- D1 "212.55 vs 209.3" is closed: same clamp; no change.
- D1 SG with a slide-jump approach: R5 has no SG because the slide boost is spent; a Unity SG model needs a slide-boost
  cooldown (~1 s from the slide end) shared by slide and SG.
- Uphill slide: a strong decel on a 30° slope (~−550 u/s² total); a design value elsewhere (one slope only).
- Reverse chains: heading limit depends on entry speed (162° walk, 157° sprint, 145° at 350).
